# ArtFlix 0.4.6

Added a × button at the right of completed, cancelled, interrupted and failed
scan banners for both Movies and TV Shows. Dismissal is remembered in the current
browser for that scan, including after refresh or switching libraries. A new scan
shows its own banner again. Running/cancelling scans keep their progress visible
and retain their separate Cancel scan action.

Dismissing a banner does not clear library data, warnings, saved scan status or
resume checkpoints. Warning reports remain available under Settings → About.
